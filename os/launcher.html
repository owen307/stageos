Welcome to StageOS

Your dedicated production operating system. Boots straight to your tools — no desktop, no distractions, no Windows updates mid-show.

🎛️
Built for the booth.
StageOS installs alongside your existing OS. At startup you choose Windows, macOS, or StageOS. When you're in StageOS, 100% of your hardware goes to the show.
