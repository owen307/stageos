Welcome to StageOS

Your dedicated production operating system — DMX, audio, video, and show control all in one place.

How do you want to run StageOS?
💾
Boot from USB Drive Recommended
Write StageOS to a USB drive (8GB+). Hold Option at startup on Mac, or F12 on Windows/PC. Works on any Intel Mac or PC — no SIP, no dual-boot setup required.
✓
💻
Dual Boot alongside macOS / Windows
Install StageOS alongside your existing OS. Choose at startup which to boot. Requires ~20GB free disk space. May need extra steps on Mac (SIP/rEFInd).
